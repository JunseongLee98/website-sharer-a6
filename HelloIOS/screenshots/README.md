# Screenshots

Add your screenshots here:

1. **emulator-screenshot.png** - Screenshot of the app running in the iPhone 15 Pro simulator
2. **device-photo.jpg** (or .png, .mov) - Photo, screenshot, or video of the app running on a physical iOS device

## How to Take Screenshots

### From Simulator
1. Run the app in the iOS Simulator
2. Click the simulator window to focus it
3. Press Cmd+S to save a screenshot
4. The screenshot will be saved to your Desktop
5. Move it to this directory and rename it appropriately

### From Device
1. Run the app on your physical iOS device
2. Take a screenshot:
   - On iPhone with Face ID: Press Side Button + Volume Up
   - On iPhone with Home Button: Press Side Button + Home Button
3. Transfer the screenshot to your computer (AirDrop, iCloud Photos, etc.)
4. Save it in this directory

Make sure to include both screenshots before submitting your homework!
